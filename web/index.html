house rules

  • 💤
    Not in the callAsleep in your bunk under fairy lights. Discord decides this, not you.
  • 👆
    In the call, you pickUse your row on the right or tap yourself in the room: 💻 working at the big table, 🎮 gaming on the couch, 🤝 meeting with Simon, 🍜 ramen at the bar or 📱 scrolling on a beanbag.
  • 🎧
    Deafen shortcutDeafening sends you to the meeting with Simon, and undeafening brings you back. Switch it off on your row if you deafen for other reasons.
  • 🔄
    Fresh startLeaving the call resets you, so next time you join you start as 💻 working.
  • 🤖
    Music botsOn the decks while they're in the call, otherwise charging on their dock.
